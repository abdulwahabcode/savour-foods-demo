Our Story

Karahi Se Table Tak

At Chauburji, Khan Baba started with one simple promise: khana waisa jo ghar jaisa lage, par zaiqa Lahore jaisa. Every karahi is cooked to order in a real iron karahi, every naan slapped fresh onto the tandoor wall, every kabab grilled over open flame.

No frozen shortcuts. No old oil. Just desi ghee, fresh meat, and recipes our ustads have guarded for years.
